I am Serhii Povarnin, a Go backend engineer from Ukraine focused on reliable services, telecom platforms, and high-load messaging systems.

My recent work has been in SMS, MNP, HLR, SMPP, SS7/SIGTRAN, service decomposition, queue-based processing, profiling, and production observability with Prometheus, Grafana, and Loki. I like backend systems where correctness, latency, graceful shutdown, operational visibility, and load behavior matter.

Before focusing on Go, I worked with Java/JAIN SLEE, C++, Python, computer vision, blockchain/file-system experiments, and IoT services. That older experience still shapes how I approach debugging, protocols, and long-lived production systems.

On GitHub I keep Go-focused learning and reference projects, including [go-microservices-starter](https://github.com/LeeDark/go-microservices-starter), [go-web-labs](https://github.com/LeeDark/go-web-labs), and [go-testing-zoo](https://github.com/LeeDark/go-testing-zoo).
